Privacy Policy

How BeeSharp: Spelling Bee Game handles information and protects your privacy.

↯

100% Offline App

Gameplay, quiz words, and scores run locally on your device without server transmission.

No Login Required

Ads & In-App Purchases

Monetized securely through Google AdMob ads and Google Play Billing for in-app purchases.

01

Information Collection & Usage

BeeSharp : Spelling Bee Game is designed with user privacy as a foundational principle. Because the app operates entirely offline:

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal identifying information (such as your name, email, phone number, or precise location).
  • Your spelling progress, high scores, game statistics, and user preferences are stored strictly and securely on your local device storage.
  • No account creation or login is required to access any feature of the application.
02

Google AdMob Advertising

To keep the app free and accessible to students and spelling enthusiasts, BeeSharp displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

  • AdMob may use device identifiers, advertising IDs (such as Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and interaction data to serve contextual or personalized advertisements.
  • Google AdMob operates under its own privacy policy. You can manage or reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings (Google Settings > Ads).
  • For more information on how Google uses data when you use our app, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms.
03

Google In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

BeeSharp offers optional digital goods and upgrades (such as unlocking special spelling categories or premium hints) via Google Play In-App Billing.

  • All financial transactions, payment processing, and billing details are handled directly and securely by Google Play.
  • We do not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank details, or financial credentials on any server.
  • Purchase validation is handled locally or via secure Google Play APIs on your device.
04

Children's Privacy

BeeSharp : Spelling Bee Game is suitable for learners, students, and children of all ages. We comply fully with the Google Play Families Policy and COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

05

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect updates in app features or legal compliance. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
